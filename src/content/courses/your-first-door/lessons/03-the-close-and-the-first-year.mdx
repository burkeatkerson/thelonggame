The close is a calendar, not a drama: inspection, appraisal, insurance,
underwriting conditions, final walk, keys. Each has a deadline and each
deadline has a person. Get their names in week one.

## The first year is the real course

Everything before the close was theory. The first twelve months of ownership
are where the education actually happens, and the tuition is only capped if
you do two things:

- **Track real expenses against your underwriting, monthly.** The gap between
  projected and actual is the most valuable dataset you will ever build — it
  is what you will underwrite every future deal with.
- **Touch your reserves for nothing but the property.** Six months of debt
  service is not savings. It is the wall between a bad month and a forced
  sale.

## Graduating

You clear this gate — and this course — when the property has run twelve
months on real numbers and you still want the next one. That appetite,
informed by actual expenses and an actual tenant, is what Year 4 is built on.
